CONFORMATIONAL UNCERTAINTY OF THE FOUR GLYCOL REFERENCE SIGMA-PROFILES,
AND WHAT IT DOES TO THE GLYCOL-ETHER SEPARATION MARGIN
2026-08-11.  Pre-registered in PREREGISTRATION.json before any QM job was launched;
one amendment, recorded in AMENDMENTS.json before any rescoring number existed.

THE CONVENTION
--------------
delta = ||a-b||_2 / ||b||_2 on the AREA-WEIGHTED psigmaA 51-vector (units A^2, summing
to the cavity area).  This is the convention of the tolerance curve (tol2.py displaces
the raw second column of the UD .sigma file) and of the UD file's own header ("Rows are
given as: sigma [e/A^2] ... then psigmaA [A^2]").

The existing 8-molecule ORCA campaign quoted its departures on the SHAPE-NORMALISED
profile (p/p.sum(), summing to 1).  Both were recomputed from that campaign's own .cpcm
files before any threshold was read:

    ensemble vs lowest conformer, 8 flexible molecules
        AREA-WEIGHTED    median 0.0603   range [0.0319, 0.1460]
        SHAPE-NORMALISED median 0.0599   range [0.0300, 0.1461]   <- the published one
    ethane-1,2-diol weight-free max between any two conformers
        AREA-WEIGHTED    0.4415          SHAPE-NORMALISED 0.4437  <- the quoted 0.444

The two conventions differ only through conformer-to-conformer cavity-area variation,
which is 0.7-2.7% here, so they agree to ~0.001 and the campaign's numbers are readable
against the tolerance curve's thresholds.  This was measured, not assumed.

STEP 4 -- THE NULLS
-------------------
(a) RIGID-SOLUTE CONTROL: FAIL at its pre-registered threshold.  At the delta the primary
    substitution imposes (delta* = 0.0424, the four-glycol mean), displacing the 19 rigid
    solutes moves the margin by median |d| 0.3911 (random) and 0.0708 (broadening),
    against 0.3579 and 0.0265 for the 4 solvents -- ratios 1.09 and 2.67, both above the
    pre-registered bar of 0.5.  The pre-registered consequence and the revision recorded
    against it are in RESULTS.json ("null_a_as_pre_registered"); in short, the FAIL
    stands and the tolerance curve is not a chemistry-specific instrument, but Step 3
    substitutes a measured displacement rather than reading a position off that curve, so
    what the FAIL bounds is the generality of the conclusion, not its value.
    Post-hoc and controlled (step4a): the pre-registered statistic adds a systematic shift
    to a spread, and the arms displace 4 profiles against 19.  At a matched count of four
    the ratio is 0.30; the solvent arm carries a systematic shift of -0.358 that the
    solute arms do not (-0.051 at 19 profiles, +0.026 at 4).  No matched comparison is
    clean -- each solvent enters ~45 of the 182 rows and each solute a median of 6.

(b) RANDOM DIRECTION AT EQUAL NORM: PASS.  At delta* = 0.0424 the random family's margin
    is +1.678 [5th, 95th: +1.520, +1.807] over 60 draws.  The measured physical
    displacement gives +2.270 -- above the family's 95th percentile, and moving the margin
    in the opposite direction.

(c) The chemistry-blind relabelling null,
    scripts/analysis/run_b_insuff_map_multiplicity_null.py, applies unchanged and was not
    re-run.

DESCRIPTIVE, NO THRESHOLD ATTACHED
----------------------------------
cos(measured ensemble displacement, equal-norm Gaussian broadening) = +0.040 (tri),
+0.299 (tetra), +0.125 (di).  The broadening family the tolerance curve used as its
"physical direction" is close to orthogonal to the direction a real Boltzmann ensemble
moves in; it accounts for at most 9% of the measured displacement's variance.  The
delta = 0.35 broadening threshold was therefore never the threshold this question needed.

VERDICT
-------
SURVIVES.  Every glycol's conformational displacement lands in the pre-registered small
band, and substituting all four measured ensembles raises the margin from +2.036 to
+2.270 with a 90% interval of [+1.358, +3.289], entirely above zero and above the printed
lower bound of +1.254.
