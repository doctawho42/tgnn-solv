This document is the Supporting Information for the article named above. Its own
sections, tables, figures, equations and lemmas carry an S---\S\ref{sec:si-methods},
Table~\ref{tab:si-arms}, Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}, Eq.~\eqref{eq:cosmo},

state: every claim, every set it is measured on, its uncertainty and its scope are in
the article's own text, and what this document adds is the proof, the per-row table,
the robustness grid, the control and the negative behind each.
