\noindent
This document is the Supporting Information for the article named above. Its own
sections, tables, figures, equations and lemmas carry an S---\S\ref{sec:si-methods},
Table~\ref{tab:si-arms}, Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}, Eq.~\eqref{eq:cosmo},
Lemma~\ref{prop:ident}---and a reference without one, such as \S\ref{sec:measure} or
Table~\ref{tab:baselines}, is to the article, which defines the terminology used here---including
IDAC, infinite-dilution activity coefficient---in the text and indexes it in
Table~\ref{tab:notation}. Nearly every result here is one
the article states, and for those the claim, the set it is measured on, its uncertainty and its
scope are in the article's own text, while what this document adds is the proof, the per-row table,
the robustness grid, the control and the negative behind each. The rest are these, listed so that
nobody has to find them by being surprised. One is a result the article does not draw on: the
loss-simplification ablation of \S\ref{sec:si-methods}, a pre-registered check that the paradox is
not an artifact of the objective, whose numbers appear in this document alone and which contradicts
nothing the article states. Two are quantities an article sentence rests on and does not itself
print: the two biases of the chemistry-blind multiplicity null (\S\ref{sec:si-null}), and
limitations~(ix), (x) and~(xiii) (\S\ref{sec:si-limitations}). One is a result the article prints
without its qualification: the crystal-grounding attempt, which the article records as having
tested nothing, its pool being melting points the model already held---where that pool was new the
crystal head moved, and the downstream solubility gap has an interval crossing zero
(\S\ref{sec:e2}). For those four, this document is not optional.
