\noindent
This document is the Supporting Information for the article named above. Its own
sections, tables, figures, equations and lemmas carry an S---\S\ref{sec:si-methods},
Table~\ref{tab:si-arms}, Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}, Eq.~\eqref{eq:cosmo},
Lemma~\ref{prop:ident}---and a reference without one, such as \S\ref{sec:measure} or
Table~\ref{tab:baselines}, is to the article, which defines the terminology used here---including
IDAC, infinite-dilution activity coefficient---in the text and indexes it in
Table~\ref{tab:notation}. What this document adds to the article is evidence and not claim: the
proof, the per-row table, the robustness grid, the control and the negative behind a statement the
article has already made in its own text, with the set it is measured on, its uncertainty and its
scope. That division is a rule the two documents are held to and not a property either can certify
of itself. It was audited claim by claim against the compiled article, each failure resolved by
moving the missing quantity into the article rather than by qualifying the sentence here; so a
claim of the article whose set, uncertainty or scope prints only in this
document is an error in this submission and not a division of labour between the two.
One result here is not a claim of the article at all, and is declared rather than left
implicit: the loss-simplification ablation of \S\ref{sec:si-methods}, a pre-registered check
that the paradox is not an artifact of the objective, whose numbers appear in this document alone,
which contradicts nothing the article states and which no article sentence rests on.
