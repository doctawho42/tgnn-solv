\subsection{A second chemical closure: pKa through a Hammett LFER}
\label{sec:pka}
The machinery above is not specific to COSMO-SAC. We instantiate it in a second real
chemical domain---aqueous $\mathrm pK_a$ of substituted aromatics---where the fixed closure
is a Hammett linear-free-energy relationship (LFER) $g(\sigma)=\mathrm pK_{a,0}-\rho\,\sigma$
(with $\mathrm pK_{a,0},\rho$ the per-scaffold Hansch--Leo--Taft reaction-series constants) over
the physical intermediate $\sigma$---here the Hammett substituent constant, \emph{not} the
$\sigma$-profile of \S\ref{sec:setup}---for which Hansch--Leo tabulated constants provide an
external oracle $\zstar$. The analogy is tight: as with the
$\sigma$-profile through COSMO-SAC, a learnable physical descriptor is pushed through a fixed,
approximate, differentiable map. The two classic LFER failure modes fall cleanly onto the two
error channels of Lemma~\ref{prop:decomp}: \emph{resonance saturation}---a curvature in
$\sigma$ that the linear closure structurally cannot represent---is a mis-map of a function of
$\zstar$ and lands in $\Bclos$; \emph{ortho/steric} field effects, absent from the tabulated
constants, are information the intermediate lacks and land in $\Binsuf$. The chemical scope is a
fidelity sweep: meta/para monosubstituted benzoic acids, phenols, and anilinium ions are the
near-exact ($\Bclos\!\approx\!0$) regime, ortho (insufficiency-dominated) and polyfunctional the low-fidelity one.

This decomposition is directly visible before any training. Pushed through the fixed Hammett
closure, $p$-nitrophenol reads $\mathrm pK_a\!\approx\!8.3$ against an experimental $\approx7.1$
(and $p$-nitroaniline overshoots similarly): the linear closure is off by $\sim1$ unit exactly
where through-conjugation ($\sigma^-$) breaks it---$\Bclos$ in the wild.

A controlled semi-synthetic construction on real tabulated $\sigma$ (known ground-truth
channels) validates the estimator and reproduces the paper's central distinction in this second
closure (Fig.~\ref{fig:pka}). Sweeping closure fidelity with a sufficient intermediate, the
grounding gap $\Gamma$ tracks $\Bclos$ (the plug-in estimate recovers the truth to within a few
percent, e.g.\ $0.20$ vs $0.21$, $0.81$ vs $0.81$), the case where Theorem~\ref{thm:gap}'s
proxy is valid. Sweeping the ortho/steric channel with a \emph{well-specified} closure gives
$\Bclos=0$ throughout yet $\Gamma>0$ and growing---the pKa realization of the conflation that
makes $\Gamma$ a symptom rather than a certificate. The oracle-$\sigma$ pipeline
(scaffold-resolved substituent$\to\sigma$ assignment) and this validation extend directly to real
data. On the OPERA experimental $\mathrm pK_a$ compilation \cite{mansouri2019opera} ($6{,}502$
compounds), applying the same decomposition to the fixed Hammett closure yields a \emph{measured}
fidelity sweep: on the clean meta/para series ($n{=}161$) the closure predicts experimental
$\mathrm pK_a$ to RMSE $0.63$ with $\Bclos$ below the input-insufficiency floor (per-scaffold
systematic offsets $\le0.25$, so the LFER is well specified there), while the
ortho/heteroaromatic regime ($n{=}1008$) degrades to RMSE $2.4$, dominated by input insufficiency
($\Binsuf\approx4.7$---the tabulated $\sigma$ carries no ortho information, exactly the a-priori
channel assignment) with an additional closure misspecification $\Bclos\ge1.1$ on top. This is the
near-exact-to-degraded transition the closure is meant to show, now measured rather than asserted. This places the pKa closure near the phase-diagram boundary
in its clean regime. We close the loop with a trained comparison. On a Bemis--Murcko scaffold
split of the Hammett-amenable set ($1169$ compounds, three seeds), a shared GIN encoder feeding
the fixed LFER (a predicted $\sigma$) beats the matched DirectGNN control overall ($1.63$ vs
$1.84$ pKa MAE, $\Delta=-0.21$), placing pKa on the ``helps'' side of the phase picture
(Fig.~\ref{fig:phase}). We read this cautiously, \emph{not} as a clean confirmation of the sign
rule through closure fidelity. Stratified, the help concentrates in the ortho/heteroaromatic
regime ($\Delta=-0.53$), not the clean meta/para one ($\Delta=-0.10$, within seed noise); and on
the clean regime the fixed LFER on the \emph{true} $\sigma$ is excellent (oracle MAE $0.60$) yet
the trained arm is not ($1.61$), so the closure is well specified there but the small-data encoder
cannot learn $\sigma$ well enough to exploit it. Where the physics arm does win---ortho, where the
closure is \emph{mis}specified for the true $\sigma$---the \emph{freely} learned $\sigma$ can
absorb ortho information the tabulated Hammett constant lacks and the per-scaffold $\mathrm pK_{a,0}$
supplies a strong baseline. So the pKa ``help'' is a permissive-closure and per-scaffold-prior
effect, not the variance-saving of a well-specified prior: a useful caution that a ``physics
helps'' outcome can be the encoder working through a soft closure. The absolute errors
($\sim\!1.6$--$1.9$) are weak for pKa and the DirectGNN control is not separately tuned, so we do
not lean on this comparison---the robust second-domain result is the decomposition above; the
trained comparison is a cautionary complement.
