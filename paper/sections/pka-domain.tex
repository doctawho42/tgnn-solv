\subsection{A second chemical closure: pKa through a Hammett LFER}
\label{sec:pka}
The machinery is not specific to COSMO-SAC. In a second real domain---aqueous $\mathrm pK_a$ of
substituted aromatics---the fixed closure is a Hammett linear-free-energy relationship
$g(\sigma)=\mathrm pK_{a,0}-\rho\,\sigma$ over the Hammett substituent constant $\sigma$ (\emph{not}
the $\sigma$-profile of \S\ref{sec:setup}), for which Hansch--Leo tabulated constants supply the
external oracle. The two classic LFER failure modes fall cleanly onto the two channels of
Lemma~\ref{prop:decomp}: \emph{resonance saturation} (a curvature the linear closure cannot
represent) lands in $\Bclos$, while \emph{ortho/steric} effects, absent from the tabulated
constants, are information the intermediate lacks and land in $\Binsuf$. On the OPERA experimental
compilation \cite{mansouri2019opera} this yields a \emph{measured} fidelity sweep: the clean
meta/para series ($n{=}161$) is well specified (RMSE $0.63$, $\Bclos$ below the insufficiency floor),
while the ortho/heteroaromatic regime ($n{=}1008$) degrades to RMSE $2.4$, input-insufficiency
dominated---the near-exact-to-degraded transition, measured rather than asserted. A trained physics
arm (learned $\hat\sigma$ through the fixed LFER) beats its matched DirectGNN control overall
($1.63$ vs $1.84$ pKa MAE), but the informative test is the same oracle swap as on solubility, now
run per stratum: replace the learned $\hat\sigma$ with the tabulated $\sigma^\star$ in the same
closure and read the sign of the change. It \emph{flips}. On the clean meta/para series feeding the
reference constant \emph{helps}---MAE $1.61\to0.60$, oracle below learned in every seed---so there the
ceiling is the noisy learned estimate, not the closure: a real-data \emph{grounding-helps} corner and
an internal positive control, the swap reading correctly where the latent is physical. On the ortho
regime the same swap \emph{hurts}---MAE $1.39\to1.62$, oracle above learned in every seed---because
the freely learned $\hat\sigma$ absorbs the ortho/steric information the tabulated constant lacks,
the compensating-surrogate mechanism of \S\ref{sec:surrogate} on a second closure. The ortho gap is
small ($+0.23$\,MAE, with the oracle evaluated on a single split), so we lean on the sign flip and
its strong meta/para pole, not the ortho magnitude. Either way the diagnostic is not an artifact of
solubility: on an independent chemical map it separates a helps-corner from a hurts-corner, measured.
The full construction, the semi-synthetic estimator validation, the measured OPERA sweep, and the
stratified comparison are in App.~\ref{sec:si-pka} (Fig.~\ref{fig:pka}).
