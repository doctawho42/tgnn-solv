\paragraph{The unconstrained control's representation.} If the physics arm's intermediate is a
surrogate, the thermodynamics might still be present implicitly in the control, which is free to
represent it. It is absent. A ridge probe of the control's pair representation recovers the model's
own prediction at $R^{2}=0.98$ and leaves the measured ideal-solubility term unrecovered, scored on
$13$ held-out solutes over $527$ rows at zero solute overlap; that term is $92.2\%$ between-solute
variance, so the effective $n$ is $13$ clusters. The three seeds give
$R^{2}=-0.506/{+}0.111/{+}0.027$, with permutation $p=0.894/0.055/0.177$ against a $1000$-draw null,
and their between-seed range of $0.616$ exceeds any one of the three, so a single seed would have
been unreadable and seed $43$ alone would have read as a near-miss. The negative therefore falls
short of an exclusion, and the level below which it excludes nothing is uncomputed. What the deposit
fixes is the null's shape: each measured value sits at or below its own null's $95$th percentile
($0.105/0.117/0.096$), which is what the three $p$ say in another currency, so the design excludes
nothing below that ceiling and we ran no power calculation to say how far above it the design
reaches. We draw no comparison between the between-seed spread and the null's support, the null
being two-sided and reaching below the lowest measured value.

That is limitation~(ix), and two things fix its size rather than the encoder's: the $13$ solutes are
capped by the reference scarcity of \S\ref{sec:data}, and the crystal-slope test is capped by the
corpus, whose empirical $\lnx$-against-$1/T$ slope carries $-\dHfus/\Rgas$ at only $r=+0.24$, so the
quantity the probe is asked to find is itself weakly present in the labels it is scored against.
What the representation is organised by instead is scaffold identity, whose share of the
representation's variance sits at the solute-identity ceiling---weak evidence of within-scaffold
indistinguishability and not a measurement of it, since the two partitions nearly coincide by
construction on this split, $11$ of the $124$ test scaffolds holding more than one solute and the
two agreeing on $91\%$ of the data. A test molecule on an unseen scaffold is then a point off the
axis the representation is organised along, which describes this representation and does not
diagnose the encoder, a diagnosis the linear probe of \S\ref{sec:supporting} leaves unsupplied. The
full tables, the controls, the direct within-scaffold measure on the solutes that do share one, and
the temperature probe are in \S\ref{sec:si-blackbox}.
